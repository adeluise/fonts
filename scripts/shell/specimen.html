The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

This page has no app behind it, so it stands in for one. Everything here exists to give the typeface something to do — a headline that runs to two lines, body copy long enough to judge rhythm, a list, and a line of code.

Setting body copy

Type is easy to judge badly. A face that looks striking in a specimen can fall apart at fourteen pixels, and a face that looks dull in isolation often turns out to be the one you can read for an hour without noticing it. The only honest test is a paragraph at the size you'll actually ship.

Watch the spacing between words as much as the letterforms. If the gaps start to feel uneven, or the line begins to look like a row of vertical strokes, the face is fighting the measure rather than supporting it.

A few things to check

  • Numerals: 0123456789 — do they line up in a column?
  • Capitals against lowercase: Hamburgefonstiv
  • Punctuation weight: “quotes,” semicolons; parentheses (like this)
  • Inline monospace against the body text around it

Smaller sizes

Captions, footnotes and metadata live down here. This is where high-contrast display faces tend to break down — the thin strokes thin out until they disappear, and what read as elegant at sixty pixels reads as broken at fourteen.
